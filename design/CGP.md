# The Crush Guy — CGP (corporate design guidelines)

Status: **v1** (TASK-003, 2026-10-29). Decisions: ADR-009 (logo), ADR-023 (fonts), ADR-029 (palette A "Midnight coral").
Tokens: `design/tokens.json` = source of truth, mirrored in `src/app/globals.css` `@theme` (guarded by `src/lib/design/tokens.test.ts`).

## 1. Logo
- Files: `design/logo/` (SVG masters + PNG exports), regenerated by `design/logo/generate.py` — never hand-edit.
- Versions: horizontal (default), horizontal on dark, mono ink, mono white, stacked, icon, simple icon (≤ 48 px), app icons (blush / ink).
- Clear space: height of the heart's glasses bar on every side. Minimum width: horizontal 120 px; icon 16 px (use the simple icon below 48 px).
- Don't: stretch, rotate, recolour the heart outside the palette, add a body or a face, put the mascot in suggestive poses, place on busy photos.

## 2. Colour (palette A — Midnight coral)
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `ink` | #1B1530 | text, dark surfaces, score badge |
| `ink-soft` | #3D3550 | secondary dark surfaces |
| `muted` | #5A5370 | secondary text |
| `coral` | #FF4D6D | logo heart, score bars, decoration — **never** small text or behind white text (3.2:1) |
| `coral-strong` | #C81E45 | text accents on light backgrounds |
| `cta` / `cta-hover` | #C81E45 / #A8163A | primary CTA ("Visit {App}") with white text (5.6:1 / 7.4:1) |
| `blush` | #FFE4EA | chips, avatar tiles, soft highlights |
| `paper` | #FFF8F9 | page background |
| `lavender` | #F3F0FA | bands, footer |
| `success` / `warning` / `danger` / `info` | #047857 / #B45309 / #B91C1C / #1D4ED8 | states (all ≥ 5:1 on white) |

Every text/background pair used in the UI is listed in `tokens.json → contrast.pairs` and must reach WCAG AA 4.5:1 (test). Dark mode: not in v1 (the site is light-only); add a mapping before enabling it.

## 3. Typography
- **Fraunces** (variable wght + opsz, OFL) — headings, wordmark, scores; ExtraBold 800 for H1/H2.
- **Onest** (variable wght, OFL) — body and UI; 400 text, 500–600 labels/buttons.
- Self-hosted woff2, latin + latin-ext (ADR-014/023). No Cyrillic/Greek in Fraunces — decide a fallback before such a locale.
- Scale (Tailwind): H1 `text-3xl` → `sm:text-4xl/5xl`, H2 `text-2xl`, H3 `text-xl`, body `text-base`, meta `text-sm`/`text-xs`. Line height: relaxed for long text.

## 4. Spacing & layout
4 px grid (Tailwind spacing). Container `max-w-5xl` (≈ 1024 px) with 16 px side gutters; reading pages `max-w-2xl`. Breakpoints: Tailwind defaults (`sm` 640, `md` 768, `lg` 1024).

## 5. Components
- **Primary CTA**: pill (`radius-pill`), `bg-cta` → `hover:bg-cta-hover`, white semibold text, label "Visit {App}", always `rel="sponsored nofollow"` to `/go/…`.
- **App card (row)**: white, `radius-card`, 1 px `ink/10` border, rank in Fraunces muted, avatar tile (blush + coral-strong initial), score badge, review link, CTA.
- **Score badge**: `ink` background, white Fraunces number with one decimal + "/10" in muted white.
- **Rating bar**: `blush` track, `coral` fill (decorative, aria-hidden; the number is shown as text).
- **Chips/tags**: `blush` or `lavender` pill, ink text.
- **Age gate box**: bordered neutral box, SFW copy, one button "I am 18 or older".
- **"Sponsored" label** (when paid slots exist): text label "Sponsored" on a `lavender` chip, never inside ranked lists (ADR-005).
- **Disclosure line**: `text-xs muted` near the top of every money page.

## 6. Iconography
None in v1 (text + emoji-free UI). When needed: Lucide, 1.5 px stroke, `currentColor`.

## 7. Imagery
Own app screenshots only (SFW, in a device frame), abstract/illustrative visuals, the mascot. No stock photos of people in suggestive contexts, no real persons, no vendor marketing images.

## 8. Voice & tone
Direct, warm, a little playful; plain words, short sentences; honest about prices and dark patterns; never explicit on public pages; never "AI is better than real people".
- Do: "Free plan is tiny — 20 messages a day." · "We paid for Pro so you don't have to."
- Don't: "The #1 hottest AI girlfriend!!!" · invented numbers or testimonials.
Microcopy: CTA "Visit {App}"; empty list "No apps in this category yet."; 404 "This crush ghosted us."

## 9. Social templates (to do when socials are claimed)
Avatar = app icon (blush); banner = horizontal logo on `paper` with tagline in Fraunces; post card = app name + score badge + 3 pros on `paper`.
