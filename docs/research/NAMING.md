### Language notes (agent knowledge, not native check)
- **swoon** (EN: to be overwhelmed by love/admiration) — no known meaning in DE, FR, ES, IT, PT, PL, NL, SL; JA/KO only as loanword reading. Spelling after hearing: easy in EN, mostly ok elsewhere ("sun"/"svun" mishearing risk in SL/DE).
- **ami** — FR "friend" (and ES amigo / IT amico echo it): a plus. JA 網 "net": neutral.
- **woo** — EN "to court". No negative meanings found; KO 우 (wu) neutral.
- **scout / dex / rank** — widely understood as English loanwords.

## Top 3 recommendation

1. **Swoondex.com** — "the index of swoon-worthy AI companions". Playful, warm, SFW, 8 letters, 2 syllables, clearly a *directory* (dex = index) and not an app, so it won't be mistaken for something we list. .com and .app both free. Best fit for "smart, warm, a bit playful". Risk: "swoon" is used by a dating wingman tool → run EUIPO/USPTO for SWOON in classes 9/35/42/45.
2. **Amiscout.com** — "we scout your AI friend". Strongest meaning ("ami" = friend in FR, recognizable in ES/IT/PT). Risk: "Ami" is already an AI companion app name (Ami.ai, Ami Emotional Chat) — the brand could look affiliated with one listed app. Choose only if trademark search is clean.
3. **Wooscout.com** — "scouting who to woo". Short and fun; reads as guide/tester. Weaker: "woo" is less known outside EN; spelled-out it can look like "woos-cout".
