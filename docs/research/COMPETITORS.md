# Competitors (TASK-005)

Checked 2026-10-29 · 13 sites from WebSearch results for 30 head keywords + 3 extra searches. Profiles: `raw/kw/competitors.json`. Values as observed on the opened page (no traffic estimates).

## Landscape in one paragraph
The result pages are weak: many results are parasite pages on unrelated or hijacked domains (`.edu`/`.gov` subdomains, local newspapers, SaaS blogs, throwaway `*.pages.dev` / `*.it.com` sites). Dedicated directories exist but almost none show dates, sources or a checkable method; prices are missing or contradictory; privacy is copied from vendor claims. One big list site openly sells top positions. **Our gap:** sourced, dated facts (prices, free-plan limits, privacy) on every page, a published scoring formula, privacy as a scored criterion, honest "not tested yet" labels, and rankings that can't be bought.

## Worth copying
- Dated updates and cited sources per page (fast.io, startuphub.ai).
- Starting-price column for every app in the comparison table (riverfronttimes).
- Annual-price normalisation (startuphub.ai).
- One page per app + programmatic tag pages capture long-tail queries (alternativeto, topai.tools) — matches our app/category/alternatives templates.
- Filterable directory tags: free plan, voice, image generation, adult content (itechguides) — our category lists do this from facts.

## Never copy
Paid placement inside rankings, unsourced "5,430 ratings"-style numbers, templated identical sub-scores, NSFW "Editor's choice" on indexable pages.
